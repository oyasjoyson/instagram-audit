⚠ About "who blocked me"

There's no legitimate way to see who has blocked you — Instagram doesn't expose that anywhere, including in the official data export. Tools that claim otherwise are guessing, usually by treating a disappeared follower as a block when it's just as often an unfollow, a deactivated account, or a rename.

What this tool shows honestly: who you've blocked (above, if uploaded), and the real follow-back gaps between your two lists.

How this actually works
No backend means no server ever sees your export.

Parsed locally

Files are read with the browser's built-in FileReader and compared in memory — no fetch, no upload, no exceptions.

Nothing stored

Refresh the page and everything's gone. No database, no cookies tied to your usernames, no account required.

Verifiable

Open your browser's Network tab before uploading — you'll see zero outbound requests during analysis.
